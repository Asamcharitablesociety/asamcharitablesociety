In Loving Memory

Late Sri Asam Surendra Reddy

Asam Charitable Society is being run in the loving memory of Late Sri Asam Surendra Reddy.

Inspired by his values of humanity, kindness and service, we continue his vision through educational, healthcare and community development initiatives.

Every activity of our society stands as a humble tribute to his noble life and legacy.
