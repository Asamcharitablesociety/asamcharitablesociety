Event Gallery & Stories

At Asam Charitable Society, we regularly conduct community welfare activities to support underprivileged families, students, and individuals in need. Our initiatives focus on education, food assistance, and social welfare.

To encourage education among children from economically weaker backgrounds, our volunteers distributed books, notebooks, pens, and other learning materials.

  • Educational materials distributed to students
  • Supported children's learning journey
  • Encouraged literacy and school participation

Clothes Distribution Program

As part of our social welfare efforts, clothes were distributed to families in need. The program was conducted to provide support and care to underprivileged members of the community.

  • Clothing assistance provided to needy families
  • Community volunteers actively participated
  • Promoted dignity and social support

Food Distribution Program

Essential food supplies were distributed to individuals and families facing difficulties. This initiative aimed to support those in need and promote community well-being.

  • Food packets distributed to beneficiaries
  • Supported vulnerable households
