Event Gallery & Stories

At Asam Charitable Society, we regularly conduct community welfare activities to support underprivileged families, students, and individuals in need. Our initiatives focus on education, food assistance, and social welfare.

Books & Stationery Distribution

As part of our educational support initiative, free books, notebooks, pens, and study materials were distributed to students from underprivileged backgrounds, helping them continue their education with confidence.

  • Educational kits distributed to school students
  • Supported learning and literacy development
  • Encouraged continued participation in education

Clothes Distribution Program

The society organized a clothing distribution program to support needy families and individuals. Essential clothing items were provided to promote dignity, comfort, and social welfare within the community.

  • Clothing assistance provided to deserving families
  • Community volunteers actively participated
  • Promoted care, dignity, and social support

Food Distribution Program

Essential food supplies were distributed to families facing financial hardship. This initiative aimed to reduce hunger and provide timely support to vulnerable members of the community.

  • Food packets distributed to beneficiaries
  • Supported vulnerable households
  • Encouraged volunteerism and community service
